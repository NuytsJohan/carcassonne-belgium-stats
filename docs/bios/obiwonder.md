## Bio

Johan Nuyts, known online as **obiwonder**, is the **reigning Belgian champion** and has now won the Belgian Carcassonne Championship four times (2006, 2007, 2017 and 2026). He has been part of the Belgian elite for twenty years: he won the very first Belgian championship in 2006 and reclaimed the title in 2026, a year after finishing runner-up.

In 2026 he represents Belgium at the World Championship for the **fifth time**; his best result so far is **6th place in 2021**. He has been a regular on the national team since 2021, with 48 international matches. He ranks **3rd in the Belgian ranking** and has played more than 3,300 rated games on Board Game Arena, with a peak rating of 672.

Carcassonne runs in the family. His son Wolf Nuyts (**[Sicarius Lupus](sicarius-lupus.md)**) finished runner-up behind his father at the 2017 Belgian Championship, joined him at the 2017 World Championship, and is also a member of the Belgian national team.
